Kairos Privacy Policy

Updated: November 3, 2026 · Contact: ababdotai@gmail.com

This policy describes the Kairos Chrome extension. Kairos helps you take timestamped notes and screenshots on YouTube and Bilibili and optionally use an AI provider to understand and summarize those notes.

Information the extension handles

Kairos accesses supported video pages to show its interface and use video titles, URLs/identifiers, playback timestamps, available subtitles, selected video frames, and text you enter into its note editor. It does not implement general browsing-history collection, advertising tracking, or analytics reporting to a Kairos server.

Storage and retention

Notes, screenshot images and associated video metadata are stored in the extension's local IndexedDB. Summary drafts and interface preferences may also use local browser storage. Model settings, language preferences and API keys use chrome.storage.sync; when Chrome sync is enabled, Google may synchronize these settings, including keys, across your signed-in browsers. This is not a dedicated secret vault. Kairos does not operate its own note-sync server.

Optional AI features

When you use AI features, relevant content is sent over HTTPS directly to the configured provider: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Moonshot Kimi, or OpenRouter. A visual note request may include a selected screenshot, video title and subtitle context; subtitle analysis sends subtitle text. Summarizing saved notes sends the text notes and available context, not screenshot images. Your API key authenticates requests to that provider. Providers receive ordinary network metadata such as your IP address and process data under their own terms and privacy policies. OpenRouter may route requests to a downstream model provider. Provider retention and model-training rules depend on that service and your account settings.

Your controls

You can use manual notes without an API key, remove or replace keys in Settings, delete notes in the dashboard, and remove the extension. Clearing extension data or uninstalling removes its local data; downloaded Markdown and images remain where you saved them. Previously synchronized settings and data already sent to third-party providers are governed by Google and those providers; deleting a local note does not retract an earlier AI request.

Sharing and data use

Export creates a file or copies content for you to share. Kairos does not automatically publish your notes to a community or social network. Exported material can include video content and personal text, so review it before sharing. Kairos does not sell user data or use it for personalized advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions. Data is used to provide the note-taking features described above.

Updates and contact

This policy may change as the extension evolves. The date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, contact ababdotai@gmail.com.

Kairos 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日。联系邮箱：ababdotai@gmail.com。

Kairos 读取 YouTube、哔哩哔哩视频页面中的标题、网址、时间点、可用字幕、所选画面及你输入的笔记，用于截图、记录、回看及可选的 AI 辅助功能。不实现全网浏览历史采集、广告追踪或向 Kairos 服务器上报分析数据。

笔记、截图和视频信息保存在扩展本地 IndexedDB 中；总结草稿和部分偏好也使用本地浏览器存储。模型设置、语言偏好和 API 密钥使用 Chrome 同步存储，开启同步后可能通过 Google 账号同步到其他浏览器，并非专用密钥保险库。Kairos 没有自营笔记同步服务器。

使用 AI 功能时，相关内容通过 HTTPS 直接发送至你选择的 Google Gemini、OpenAI、Anthropic、DeepSeek、Moonshot Kimi 或 OpenRouter。画面笔记可能发送所选截图、标题和字幕上下文；字幕分析发送字幕；笔记总结发送文字笔记及上下文，不发送截图。API 密钥用于服务商鉴权。服务商也会接收 IP 等网络信息，其数据保留、训练和处理规则由其政策及你的账号设置决定；OpenRouter 可能转发至下游模型服务商。

你可以不配置密钥而仅使用手动笔记，在设置中清除密钥，在管理界面删除笔记，或卸载扩展。卸载和清理扩展数据会移除本地数据，但不会删除已导出的文件，也不会撤回已经发给服务商的请求。已同步的设置与服务商保留的数据受相关服务政策约束。

导出或复制后由你决定如何分享，Kairos 不会自动公开发布笔记。不出售用户数据，不用于个性化广告、信用评估或贷款决策。分享前请检查截图、文字及个人信息。政策如有调整会更新日期；隐私问题请联系上述邮箱。
